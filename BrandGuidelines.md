# AI Emergency Copilot — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Brand Name:

LifeBridge AI

Tagline:

"From Panic to Action in Seconds"

Mission:

To bridge the gap between human panic and life-saving systems using AI.

---

## Brand Personality

- Calm under pressure
- Trustworthy
- Fast and responsive
- Life-saving and authoritative
- Simple and human-centered

---

## Color Palette

Background Color: 
- #9B8EC7

Primary Colors:

- Red (#E53935) → Emergency, urgency
- White (#FFFFFF) → Clarity, simplicity

Secondary Colors:

- Dark Blue (#1E3A8A) → Trust, reliability
- Light Gray (#F5F5F5) → Background calmness

Status Colors:

- High Risk → Red
- Medium Risk → Orange (#FB8C00)
- Low Risk → Green (#43A047)

---

## Typography

Headings:

- Bold, large, clear
- Sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Roboto)

Body Text:

- Medium weight
- Highly readable
- Avoid medical jargon

---

## UI Design Principles

1. Minimal UI (no clutter)
2. One action at a time
3. High contrast for readability
4. Large buttons for emergency use
5. Voice-first interaction

---

# Core Screens

1. Home Screen

- Big microphone button 🎤
- "Describe the emergency"
- Upload image option

---

2. Analysis Screen

- Loading animation ("Analyzing situation...")

---

3. Result Screen

Sections:

### Risk Level

- Large colored indicator (Red/Orange/Green)

### AI Assessment

- Short explanation

### What to Do Now

- Step-by-step instructions

### Emergency Actions

- Call ambulance button
- Share location button

### Nearby Help

- Hospital suggestions

---

## Voice Design

- Calm, clear, slow speech
- No robotic tone
- Reassuring but direct

Example:
"Stay calm. Help is on the way. Follow these steps."

---

## UX Behavior

- Instant feedback (<2 seconds)
- Always show next step
- Avoid overwhelming user
- Use icons + text

---

🛡️ Trust Elements

- Show confidence level
- Use simple medical reasoning
- Avoid complex terminology

---

## Accessibility

- Large fonts
- High contrast colors
- Voice guidance support
- Multilingual capability

---

## Future Extensions

- Wearable integration
- Smartwatch alerts
- Offline emergency mode
- Family notification system

---

## Brand Promise

LifeBridge AI ensures that in critical moments, users are never alone —
they have an intelligent guide that turns confusion into action instantly.
